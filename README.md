## 𝔄𝔟𝔬𝔲𝔱 𝔐𝔢

- 🦇 At Estrella Mountain Community College I am pursuing an **Associate of Applied Science (AAS) in Cybersecurity**. My specialization tracks are **Cisco Network Administration & Security** and **Red Hat Linux System Administration**.
- 🪓 In authorized labs I build and test systems to understand how **security controls** behave under different configurations. Tracing failures to their **root causes** helps me evaluate which mitigations address the underlying weakness.
- ⚰️ To retain control of personal data I run a homelab that hosts services I use daily. **Self-hosting** keeps those services under my administration and reduces my reliance on centralized providers.
- 🪦 When assessing digital exposure I draw on **open-source intelligence (OSINT)** and **operational security (OPSEC)** to examine what information is publicly available. That assessment helps me identify disclosures I can avoid.
- 🕯️ For my portfolio labs I document configuration decisions and **validation steps** so another engineer can reproduce the build and verify the results. My approach to security is grounded in **professional ethics and moral responsibility**. I aim to design controls that protect **confidentiality and system integrity** while respecting individual privacy and autonomy.
